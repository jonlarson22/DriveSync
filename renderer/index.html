Tip: click any file row to preview it here — handy for checking “Different” photos side by side before deciding which way to copy. Videos and very large files preview as details only; use Open to view them in your usual app.

How it works: organize on your “live” drive, then copy Left → Right to its backup. Files and folders “Only in Left” can only go Left → Right, “Only in Right” only Right → Left. Selecting a 📁 folder creates it on the other side (empty folders included — so your structure can match even where no photos have landed yet). “Different” files will overwrite the other side when you copy in that direction — you'll get a warning count first. Identical items are never copied. Nothing is ever deleted. If you renamed a folder on the live drive, check the “Possible folder renames” panel above instead of copying — renaming on the backup is instant and leaves no duplicate behind.
